The pieces

annuity per unit notional A = Vfixed / (N K);   par rate R = Vfloat / (N A);   ∇XIR = ∇XIVfloat / (N A) − R ∇XIVfixed / Vfixed;   J = ∂XM/∂XI (one row per calibration instrument);   ∇XMV = ∇XIV J−1;   hedge: BT w = −rT.

The annuity is the fixed-leg PV per unit coupon and notional; the par rate is the coupon that prices the swap to zero. The par-rate gradient with respect to the internal state (the node forwards, flattened in model component order) follows from the quotient rule with the coupon held fixed, and each calibration instrument's gradient is one row of the Jacobian, which is 18 × 18 here (17 OIS nodes plus the funding node) and lower triangular on the OIS block because a quote only depends on nodes up to its own maturity. Quote risk is the chain rule across the change of coordinates from state to quotes, computed by solving JT rT = (∇XIV)T; the report scales by 10−4 to USD per basis point.

A receive-fixed swap loses when rates rise, so its net risk is negative and concentrated in the 9Y and 10Y quotes, whose forward segments carry the swap's last cashflows. Raising an earlier quote lifts the forward on its own segment and lowers the next one (the following swap must still reprice), and a swap that pays both segments sees the two moves cancel to first order, which leaves the small positive residuals at 1Y to 8Y; quotes beyond 10Y cannot move the swap at all. The hedge is about one million of pay-fixed split roughly half and half between 9Y and 10Y, a few thousand of receive-fixed at the shorter tenors, and nothing beyond 10Y. A par OIS has risk only to its own quote, equal to minus its PV01.
